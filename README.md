Workflow
===============
1. To start, [**fork** repository][forking] [github.com/utk-cs445-fall26/MiniProject1][assignment]
   1. Go to github.com/utk-cs445-fall26/MiniProject1
   1. Click on "Fork Repository" button
	1. Select your GitHubID as the organization to fork to
	1. ** Important: go to Settings and enable issue tracking**
1. Create new notebook YourUTKID.ipynb in your fork.
1. Open your notebook and add a text cell describing goals and ways to achieve your goal (data sources/comparisons)
1. save your notebook and push to your repository
1. At https://github.com/[YourGHUsername]/MiniProject1
   Create a [**pull request**][pull-request] on the
   original repository [utk-cs445-fall26/MiniProject1][assignment]  to
   turn in that initial description.
1. From your assigned group, Comment on the description produced by your peer listed to the right of your netID by creating an issue on their fork (see the groups on fdac/news)
   - if you are the last listed netID of your group, the peer you will comment on will be the first member of your group
1. Respond to issue raised by the peer appearing to the left of your netID in your fork
   - if your are the first  listed netID of your group, the peer you respond to will be the last member of your group
1. Work on the analysis and presentation


Social Workflow with deadlines
==============================

1. Do the first six steps listed above before the class on Sep 3rd: if you have any
   issues, we need to clarify them before Sep 3rd.
1. Come up with an idea of what analysis you are going to use and
   what data sources you will use. Discuss it with your assigned peer on Sep 3rd.
   You present the idea to the peer you commented on in the above section.
   You produce a comment for the peer you responded to in the above section.
   To access your peer's forked repository, manually enter the url:
   https://github.com/YourPeersGHUsername/MiniProject1

When you raise an issue, you should assign it to the GHUSER who should comment on it.
1. Start doing the analysis and discuss your progress with your peer
   so that you are done on Sep 10th. In case you encounter any problems
   retrieving, storing, or analyzing data please discuss them with
   your peer and, if needed, escalate.
1. Prepare a few slides (or a jupyter notebook) explaining the approach and findings and present to a small group as specified below.

    
At the end of the presentations within the group a vote will be held (by that group) for one presentation 
to be selected for the presentation to the entire class. The first person listed in each group will record that in [this issue](https://github.com/utk-cs445-fall26/MiniProject1/issues/1)
